Ali Footwork

The Feet

Slow technique on Wednesday, speed on Saturday. Five principles behind the movement.

  • 01Stay on the balls of your feet. Light, springy, never flat-footed.
  • 02Circle laterally, both ways. Glide side to side, not just forward and back.
  • 03In and out. Dart in to throw, dart straight back out of range.
  • 04Never cross your feet. Step and drag to keep your base.
  • 05The Ali shuffle. Quick foot-switch — flashy, but a great speed builder.

04
Eat For The Goal

Strong & Lean Fueling

Strong-and-lean is a recomp, not a bulk. You're feeding strength and recovery without burying your definition.

The Levers

  • Eat around maintenance, or a slight surplus. No need to force-feed. This is the lane where you add strength and stay lean at the same time.
  • Protein is the anchor: ~110–155 g/day (about 0.7–1.0 g per lb). High protein protects muscle in a recomp and drives strength recovery.
  • Carbs around training. Fuel the heavy compounds and the rope work — they make you perform and look fuller without much fat cost.
  • Boxing now works for you. Since you're not chasing a big surplus, the rope and footwork calories aren't fighting your goal — they're what keeps you lean.
  • Sleep is non-negotiable. Strength gains live in recovery. Two rest days plus good sleep is why the heavy lifts keep climbing.
  • Watch the calves. Rope + calf raises stack up. If the Achilles or shins complain, drop dedicated calf sets first.
  • Deload every 6–8 weeks. Halve the lifting volume for a week and keep footwork to technique only.

Protect Yourself At All Times

  • Warm up before every session and stretch after.
  • Progress gradually — overuse injuries come from doing too much too soon.
  • Technique first, speed second, power last.
  • Yoga days are rest days. Move gently, breathe, and back off any pose that pinches.
  • If you spar, do it at a real gym with supervision and headgear.
  • This is general fitness guidance, not personal-training or medical advice. Check with a professional before starting if you have any health concerns.
